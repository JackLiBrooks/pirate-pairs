# pirate-pairs

## How to Play Pirate Pairs

Pirate Pairs is a turn-based card game where you draw cards and try **not** to draw a card that matches a value already in your hand.

### Goal

Stay in the game as long as possible, and be the last one standing.

### Setup

* The deck contains numbers **1–10**.
* There are **1** card with value 1, **2** cards with value 2, … up to **10** cards with value 10 (55 cards total).
* Shuffle the deck.
* Each player starts with an empty hand and a score of 0.

### On Your Turn

1. Choose to either **take the lowest card** in someone's hand(your score increases by that many points, your hand is cleared and discarded, and the card you took from someone is discarded) or **Draw the top card** from the deck.
2. If the drawn card **does NOT match** any number already in your hand:

   * Add the card to your hand.
   * Your turn ends.
3. If the drawn card **matches** a number already in your hand (you made a pair):

   * You score points equal to the card’s value.
   * All cards in your hand (and the drawn card) go to the discard pile.
   * Your hand is cleared.
   * Your turn ends.

### Game End

* Players will be eliminated once their score passes a limit (based on the number of players).
* The game ends when only one player is still “in.”
* If multiple players are still in by the time the deck runs out, the discard pile is recycled and becomes the deck.
